COMMAND TYPE
    Deployment & Infrastructure commands

NAME
    uninstall-identity - Remove recorded local IdentityService and preserve Creatio

SYNOPSIS
    clio uninstall-identity -e ENVIRONMENT [--skip-crm-cleanup]

OPTIONS
    -e, --environment NAME
        Required explicit registered environment name.
    --skip-crm-cleanup
        Recovery only. Leave Creatio identity system settings unchanged with a warning.
        Default: false

DESCRIPTION
    Uses the environment's IdentityService attachment in clio appsettings to identify
    the exact folder, IIS target and pool. Clears matching CRM references first, removes
    the recorded artifacts, then empties the attachment and matching clio credentials.
    Preserves Creatio, its database, OAuth application records and environment entry.
    Shared pools are preserved. Unsafe or shared identity targets are refused.
    Unresolvable IIS paths must be restored or their stale mappings corrected before retrying.
    An empty attachment succeeds without deleting anything; legacy deployments must
    have their verified location recorded first. Failed cleanup retains retry metadata.
    Enable with: clio experimental --name deploy-identity --enable

EXAMPLES
    clio uninstall-identity -e local-dev
    clio uninstall-identity -e local-dev --skip-crm-cleanup

RELATED COMMANDS
    deploy-identity, uninstall-creatio
