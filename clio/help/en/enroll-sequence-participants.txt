Enroll explicit contacts using Creatio's native sequence service.
Usage: clio enroll-sequence-participants -e <environment> --sequence-id <uuid> --contact-ids <uuid>,<uuid>
Accepts 1–100 unique contact IDs. Does not activate the sequence. Native eligibility,
capacity, duplicate handling and activity creation apply. May trigger configured outreach.
Inspect completion, platform counts and readback. Added does not mean Active.
No automatic retry: uncertain completion requires record/activity verification before resubmission.
